Privacy policy

Effective October 28, 2026

In short: Jikai doesn't collect any data. There are no Jikai servers, no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Everything stays on your iPhone. The app talks to AniList and MyAnimeList on your behalf, plays trailers through YouTube, and downloads a public trailer list from this website.

What the app stores on your iPhone

  • When you sign in: your AniList and/or MyAnimeList username, and a sign-in token kept in the iOS Keychain. Jikai never sees your password.
  • A copy of your anime lists, your watch history (episodes per day, for streaks) and your settings, so the app and widgets work offline.
  • People you pinned or viewed recently on the Friends tab, people you blocked, and a short-lived copy of friends' public lists and stats for comparisons and the streak widget.
  • Saved copies of images and pages you've already seen (covers, profile pictures, Discover, favourites), so they load faster.
  • In Swipe: which shows you've already swiped past, the trailers you reported, and your subtitle and translation choices.

This information never leaves your device except as described below. It isn't sent to the developer or to anyone else.

AniList and MyAnimeList

To show and update your lists, Jikai connects directly from your iPhone to AniList (anilist.co) and MyAnimeList (myanimelist.net). These services receive the requests the app makes for you, such as reading your list or saving a change you made, and show cover images from their servers. Their own privacy policies apply:

  • AniList privacy policy and terms
  • MyAnimeList privacy policy

Trailers in Swipe

Trailers play in YouTube's embedded player. While one plays, YouTube (Google) receives the request like any embedded video, including your IP address and device information, and its own privacy policy applies. Subtitles and their automatic translation are YouTube's own features.

The list of which trailer belongs to which show is a public file on this website (hosted by GitHub Pages). Downloading it works like visiting any web page; it contains nothing about you.

Reporting a wrong trailer opens a pre-filled email in your Mail app with the show's name and the trailer link. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself.

Streaming links

A show's page can link to where it streams, such as Netflix, Crunchyroll or Bilibili. Tapping one opens that service's app or website, and its own privacy policy applies.

Posts, follows and other social features

Posts, replies, likes and follows you make in Jikai are sent directly to AniList and appear on your public AniList profile, just as if you made them on anilist.co. Jikai doesn't store or moderate them on any server of its own. Reports go to AniList's own moderation. Blocking someone in Jikai hides them only inside the app on your iPhone.

When you share something (for example a Season Finale card or a 3×3 grid), the image is made on your iPhone and goes only where you choose to share it.

Notifications and Live Activities

Episode reminders, Live Activities and the "lists synced" notification are created on your iPhone. No push notification service or server is involved.

Deleting your data and revoking access

  • Sign out of AniList or MyAnimeList in Jikai → Profile → Settings → Connections. This deletes the sign-in token from your iPhone.
  • Deleting the app removes the data it stored. iOS may keep Keychain items (the sign-in token) after an app is deleted; signing out first removes them.
  • Posts and follows live on AniList; delete them there or in Jikai.
  • You can also revoke Jikai's access in your account settings on AniList or MyAnimeList.

Children

Jikai isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect any personal information.

Changes
