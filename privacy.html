Privacy policy

Effective October 30, 2026

In short: Jikai doesn't collect data about you. There are no Jikai accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your lists and settings stay on your iPhone. The app talks to AniList and MyAnimeList on your behalf, plays trailers through YouTube, and downloads a public trailer list from this website. Suggestions, matches and games are worked out on your iPhone. Jikai's own server keeps only the cards you choose to post to AniList and, for the Jikai badge, your AniList username (see below); you can delete both in the app.

What the app stores on your iPhone

  • When you sign in: your AniList and/or MyAnimeList username, and a sign-in token kept in the iOS Keychain. Jikai never sees your password.
  • A copy of your anime and manga lists, your watch history (episodes and chapters per day, for streaks) and your settings (theme, layouts, language), so the app and widgets work offline.
  • People you pinned or viewed recently on the Friends tab (with their public AniList user ID, so their posts can be loaded), people you blocked, and a short-lived copy of friends' public lists and stats for comparisons, Guess the score and the streak widget.
  • Saved copies of images and pages you've already seen (covers, profile pictures, Discover, favourites), so they load faster.
  • In Swipe: which shows you've already swiped past, the trailers you reported, and your subtitle and translation choices.
  • From the first-run setup: the shows you said you've seen, the genres you picked and the trailers you liked, used to suggest shows.
  • From the Play tab: main characters of the shows on your list (public AniList data, saved so they load once), your This or That winners and your best Guess the score results.

This information never leaves your device except as described below. It isn't sent to the developer or to anyone else.

AniList and MyAnimeList

To show and update your lists, Jikai connects directly from your iPhone to AniList (anilist.co) and MyAnimeList (myanimelist.net). These services receive the requests the app makes for you, such as reading your list or saving a change you made, and show cover images from their servers. Their own privacy policies apply:

  • AniList privacy policy and terms
  • MyAnimeList privacy policy

Suggestions and matches

"You might like" and "Is this for me?" are worked out on your iPhone from your list and the picks above. Nothing about your taste is sent anywhere. The shows you pick in the first-run setup are only added to your AniList or MyAnimeList list if you tap "Add to my list".

Trailers

Trailers (in Swipe and the first-run setup) play in YouTube's embedded player. While one plays, YouTube (Google) receives the request like any embedded video, including your IP address and device information, and its own privacy policy applies. Subtitles and their automatic translation are YouTube's own features.

The list of which trailer belongs to which show is a public file on this website (hosted by GitHub Pages). Downloading it works like visiting any web page; it contains nothing about you.

Reporting a wrong trailer opens a pre-filled email in your Mail app with the show's name and the trailer link. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself.

Streaming and reading links

A show's page can link to where it streams, such as Netflix, Crunchyroll or Bilibili, and a manga's page to where it can be read officially, such as MANGA Plus or WEBTOON. Tapping one opens that service's app or website, and its own privacy policy applies.

Posts, follows and other social features

Posts, replies, likes and follows you make in Jikai are sent directly to AniList and appear on your public AniList profile, just as if you made them on anilist.co. Jikai doesn't store or moderate them on any server of its own. Reports go to AniList's own moderation. Blocking someone in Jikai hides them only inside the app on your iPhone.

The Following feed shows posts from people you follow on AniList and from people whose profiles you recently opened, loaded from AniList like any public page.

When you share something (for example a Season Finale or Final Chapter card, a 3×3 grid or a This or That result), the image is made on your iPhone and goes only where you choose to share it.

Cards you post to AniList

When you post a 3×3 grid, a This or That result or a Season Finale / Final Chapter card to AniList from Jikai, the picture is uploaded to Jikai's server (run on Cloudflare) so your post can show it. Only these cards are accepted, not other photos. Each picture gets a public link that anyone who sees the post can open.

With each picture the server keeps: the picture, when it was uploaded, what kind of card it is, and an anonymous ID for your copy of Jikai. That ID comes from Apple's App Attest, which confirms requests come from the real Jikai app; it isn't linked to your name, Apple ID, AniList or MyAnimeList account, and the server doesn't keep IP addresses. Cloudflare processes requests as the hosting provider under its privacy policy.

Delete every card you've posted in Jikai → Profile → Settings → Posts and sharing → Delete cards I've posted. They stop showing in your AniList posts; the posts' text stays on AniList.

The Jikai badge

So other Jikai users can see a small Jikai mark next to your name, the server keeps your AniList username (public on AniList anyway) together with the anonymous ID of your copy of Jikai. To show the marks, Jikai asks the server which of the names on screen use Jikai. Turn it off in Settings → Posts and sharing → Show the Jikai badge on my name, and your name is removed from the server.

Notifications and Live Activities

Episode reminders, Live Activities and the "lists synced" notification are created on your iPhone. No push notification service or server is involved.

Deleting your data and revoking access

  • Sign out of AniList or MyAnimeList in Jikai → Profile → Settings → Connections. This deletes the sign-in token from your iPhone.
  • Deleting the app removes the data it stored. iOS may keep Keychain items (the sign-in token) after an app is deleted; signing out first removes them.
  • Posts and follows live on AniList; delete them there or in Jikai.
  • Cards you posted from Jikai: Settings → Posts and sharing → Delete cards I've posted.
  • You can also revoke Jikai's access in your account settings on AniList or MyAnimeList.

Children

Jikai isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect any personal information.
