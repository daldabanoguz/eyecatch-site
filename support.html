Questions

Do I need an account?
No. Type your public AniList or MyAnimeList username to see your list. Sign in (Settings → Accounts) only if you want to add shows, update progress or see a private list.
My list says it's private.
Sign in with that site, or make your list public in its settings.
Can I use AniList and MyAnimeList together?
Yes. Eyecatch merges both lists and counts shows on both sites once. When you're signed in to both, changes are saved to both.
Where do streaks and binge days come from?
From your AniList activity feed. MyAnimeList doesn't share watch history, so for MyAnimeList-only users Eyecatch counts the episodes you log in the app.
I don't get episode reminders.
Check that notifications are allowed for Eyecatch in iOS Settings, and that "Episode reminders" is on in Eyecatch → Settings. Reminders arrive 1 hour before each episode of shows you're watching.
The widget shows old times.
Open Eyecatch once to refresh your list; widgets update from it.
